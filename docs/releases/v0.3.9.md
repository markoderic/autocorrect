# AutoCorrect 0.3.9 — Persistent spelling underlines (preview)

Unnotarized preview for macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. macOS may show "Apple cannot check it for malicious software" on first launch; see the README for the Open Anyway steps. This release also contains the unpublished 0.3.8 correction improvements (see `docs/releases/v0.3.8.md`).

## What changed

- **Persistent, multiple underlines.** Unresolved possible misspellings stay marked while you keep typing, several at once, until each word is corrected, deleted, ignored or deliberately retyped. The old single mark that vanished on the next keystroke or after five seconds is gone.
- **Existing text.** When a field gains focus, after pasting, scrolling or other edits, AutoCorrect checks a bounded region around the caret (the editor's visible range when it reports one, at most about 2,000 characters) and marks unresolved misspellings there. Existing text is never rewritten automatically; automatic correction still happens only for the word you just completed.
- **What is never marked:** names and other capitalized words, acronyms, mixed-case identifiers, paths, addresses, code and numbers, ignored words, words with a custom rule, recognized product/technical terms, the word being typed, and contextual grammar doubts such as `its`/`it's` (those stay in the menu only).
- **Lifecycle.** Marks follow window moves and resizes, hide briefly while you type or scroll and return once the view settles, disappear when you switch apps, and clear predictably when the toggle is turned off. The overlay is click-through and never takes focus.
- **Honest status.** The menu shows `Underlines: N shown` plus how many marks could not be drawn because the editor exposes no trustworthy geometry, or that text access is unavailable in the current app. The ChatGPT/Codex desktop composer is such a field on this Mac: errors are detected and counted, but no position is drawn.
- **Bounds.** At most 32 marks per field; one native spelling query per scanned region; all refreshes are debounced; no text is stored beyond the flagged words and their offsets.

## Verification

Unit and engine tests for the mark model, tokenization, placement with injected geometry, and engine bookkeeping. Live checks with a traced build in TextEdit and Notes (marks drawn: window-server bounds under the words and in-app bitmap render counts) and in the ChatGPT/Codex app (detected, not drawable). The underline overlay cannot be captured by the automation tool used for testing, so a visual confirmation by a person is still requested; Claude desktop and browsers were not tested. Details: `docs/VALIDATION.md`.
