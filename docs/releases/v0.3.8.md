# AutoCorrect 0.3.8 — Fewer wrong replacements (preview)

Unnotarized preview for macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. macOS may show "Apple cannot check it for malicious software" on first launch; see the README for the Open Anyway steps.

## What changed

- **Fewer wrong replacements.** When macOS recommends a different word but its own ranked guesses contain a repair that keeps every letter you typed, that repair wins (`higer → higher`, not `tiger`; `caost → coast`; `considerd → considered`). When several such repairs compete, the word is left alone. On a pinned public misspelling corpus, wrong automatic replacements fell from 118 to 77 (held-out half) and 131 to 106 (development half) on one Mac.
- **Joined phrases stay phrases.** `abit`, `aswell`, `upto`, `infront`, `sofar`, `ontop` are no longer turned into `bit`, `swell`, `unto`, `infant`, `solar`, `onto`; they are underlined and the two-word phrase is offered. `everytime`, `eachother`, `nevermind`, `ofcourse`, `infact` split automatically.
- **More contractions.** `hes`, `shes`, `itll`, `youd`, `theyd`, `wouldve`, `couldve`, `shouldve`, `aint`, `yall`, `oclock`, `wheres`, `whens` and others are repaired even when macOS offers no automatic recommendation; damaged forms of other words are no longer bent into contractions.
- **Rich editors.** Corrections that insert an apostrophe or quote are verified correctly when Notes, TextEdit, Mail or Messages convert it to a curly one, so undo and the rewrite protection keep working there. Confirmed with a traced build in TextEdit, Notes and the ChatGPT desktop app.
- **Evaluation harness.** `evaluation/` holds a pinned, licensed corpus and a gated test that reports correct repairs, misses, false corrections and abstentions separately.

## Verification

175 automated tests, 0 failures, on macOS 27.0.1. Real-editor checks used a keyboard-path-equivalent method, not a physical keyboard; Claude desktop and browsers were not tested. Details: `docs/VALIDATION.md`.
