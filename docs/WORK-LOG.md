- **Source diagnosis (before changes):** `CorrectionEngine.key()` calls `indicator.hide()` on every keystroke and clears `flaggedWord`; `checkBoundary` shows at most the latest boundary's word; `SpellingIndicator.show` hides the previous mark, owns one panel and dismisses after 5 s; `show` silently returns when the snapshot no longer matches or AX bounds/line geometry are unusable; detection happens only for the word completed at a boundary (never for existing text), and only when `assessment` marks it misspelled after ignored-word, custom, built-in, name-lexicon and manual-rewrite checks.
