# Third-party word data

Selected company, software, coding, and file-type words are derived from [CSpell dictionaries](https://github.com/streetsidesoftware/cspell-dicts) at commit `152f3ba420772105156cd0ead94a47f9ed493a8d`. The generated data filters unsupported tokens, omits ambiguous/conflicting names from automatic casing, and retains lowercase recognition terms. This app does not bundle or run CSpell itself.
